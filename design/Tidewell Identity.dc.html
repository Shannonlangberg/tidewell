the mark
A key tag. Arch top, flat bottom, one hole. The same silhouette is the app icon, the plaque by the door, and the tag on the key itself.

Construction
Height is always 1.4 × width. Top radius is always half the width — a true semicircle, never a softened rectangle. Bottom radius is fixed at 8px at 152px wide and scales with it. The hole is centred, one quarter and a half of the width across, and sits one hole-diameter from the top.
The hole is always knocked through to the surface behind. It is never filled with copper, never a ring, never an icon.

Copper rule
Copper marks one thing per screen: the decision, or the money. On bone it is a fill only — copper text must use Copper deep #8A3A12, which clears 4.5:1 on Bone, Bone light and Dune alike. On verdigris, #E2622E may only be set at 32px and above; below that use Copper bright #F07A42, which clears 4.6:1.
On copper
Text on a copper fill is always Deep #0A211C, never white — white on copper is 3.5:1 and fails. Dark green on copper is 4.8:1.
Flood rule
Verdigris floods whole sections, edge to edge, never a card or a panel floating on bone. Two floods per page at most.

06
voice
Plain, warm, first person plural. Australian English. Every screen opens with a greeting or one true sentence.
We write
"We'll be there Thursday morning."
"Approve $245"
"Nine of the next fourteen nights are booked."
"Two hours from Adelaide. Ten minutes from us."
"We answer inside the hour. We'd rather you check."
We never write
"Submit" or "Confirm" on their own
"Approx. $4.3k" — numbers in full
"Contact us for a quote"
"Shack" — it is a house, a place, a holiday home
"Leverage", "solutions", "seamless", "portfolio"
The word for the machinery. We don't name it.
Three rules
Say what happens. A button names its outcome and its amount.

Say the hard thing first. If a guest broke it, that is the first line, not the third.

One idea a screen. If there are two, there are two screens.
The opening line, by role
Owner
Nine of the next fourteen nights are booked. One thing needs you.
Guest
You're in at three. The code appears on this screen at midday.
Tenant
Rent is paid to the fourteenth. Nothing needs you today.
Manager
Three changeovers today. Wallaroo is running late.
Cleaner
Two houses today. Bayview first.
The honest lines — say these before we're asked
On the licence: "Our application for a South Australian property agent's licence is with Consumer and Business Services. Until it is granted we take on short-stay and property care only."
On breakages: "A guest broke the shower screen on Saturday. We had it measured Monday and it's in on Thursday. It comes out of the bond, and we'll show you the invoice."
On what we don't do: "We don't do rebuilds, pest control or anything that needs a licence we haven't got. We'll tell you who does."
