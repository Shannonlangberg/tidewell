# Tidewell backend design

Version 1.0, 7 October 2026. Starts from the operations proposal (highest judged score), grafts the ideas the judges flagged from the ship-fast and owner-trust proposals, fixes the fatal flaws they named, and folds in the four specs. Section 3 is written so migrations can start tomorrow. A longer working copy with every spec rule in full sits at `(working notes, not in the repo)`.

Conventions: money is integer cents (AUD); timestamps are `timestamptz`; day boundaries are evaluated in the property's timezone (`Australia/Adelaide`, DST-aware). Nothing here invents a business fact: phone, ABN, legal name, email domain and the Twilio number are `null` in `org_settings` until the founders set them, as `lib/site.ts` does today.

## 1. Summary for Shannon and Court

One database in Sydney that every screen reads and writes, replacing the demo data and browser-only state: your owners and homes, every booking from every channel, every clean and its photos, every repair with its quote and the owner's decision, every message on every channel, every statement and invoice, every door code (encrypted). The screens stay; each is rewired to real rows, and the role a person sees comes from their login, never the web address.

| Promise on the website | What makes it true |
|---|---|
| Published prices | The 18% + GST and the setup band are a `fee_schedules` row every invoice points at. |
| A live dashboard | A Hospitable webhook becomes a `reservations` row within seconds and the owner's phone refreshes live; Stayz (iCal) bookings land within 30 minutes and show as "details to come" until completed. |
| A photo of every room after every clean | A clean snapshots its rooms; the database refuses to mark it done until a photo exists for each; photos queue on the phone without reception and the owner is told only when the last one lands. |
| Replies inside the hour, 8am to 10pm | Every inbound message is a timestamped row; the first reply by a person stamps `first_human_reply_at`; you are paged at 45 minutes; breaches are counted. |
| No lock-in after the first Easter | The agreement carries `earliest_exit_date`; the owner can give notice from their phone once it passes; leaving hands back statements, invoices and photos. |
| We never hold your money | Platforms pay owners; Tidewell issues one monthly tax invoice collected by direct debit; the statement reconciles payouts against that invoice; no netted payout exists anywhere. |

Build order and what each phase makes true: 0 Foundations (one login, role from session, Sydney, audit log); 1 Owners and homes (enquiries become leads; a won lead becomes an owner with a home in onboarding); 2 Bookings in (live dashboard, calendar feed, manual bookings); 3 Turnovers and photos (cleans derived from checkouts, the offline-safe photo rule, cleaner pay lines); 4 Repairs and notifications (auditable approvals; push, SMS, email with quiet hours); 5 Stay page and door codes; 6 Inbox and the one-hour reply; 7 Money; 8 Offboarding. About 62 developer-days for one senior developer to make all six promises true for the first five owners; about 87 with the post-launch phases (voice, invoice extraction, Xero, pricing pass, lock hardware, owner-relationship triggers). Six weeks is not enough; the honest shortcut is to onboard the first owners after phase 5 and run the inbox and the first statement by hand while 6 to 8 finish.

Monthly running cost (research figures seen 7 October 2026; USD unless marked): Supabase Pro US$25 (Free while building, Pro before the first owner); Netlify Pro US$20 (Functions region `syd`); Hospitable Professional US$59 including two homes plus US$15 per extra home (US$104 at five, US$179 at ten); Twilio AU mobile US$8.25 plus US$30-70 usage (SMS US$0.0515 out, US$0.0075 in); Resend US$0 to 3,000 emails then US$20; Claude Sonnet 5.5 drafts US$5-20; Pinch about A$3 per invoice (1% + 30c capped at A$3, A$5 dishonour; Stripe BECS capped at A$3.50); Xero Grow A$78 (API free). Later: PriceLabs US$19.99 per listing, igloohome API US$2 per lock (Seam US$5), hardware A$270-360 per home. Five homes: roughly US$200-250 plus debit fees and Xero.

## 2. Decisions assumed, and the short list that needs you

The ten decisions taken: (1) Model A: platforms pay the owner; one monthly tax invoice per owner (18% + GST plus pass-throughs) collected by BECS direct debit through an abstract collector; the statement reconciles, never pays out; supplier invoices carry `addressee` and `is_disbursement`. (2) Hospitable is the launch adapter (webhooks plus nightly backfill); Tidewell-shaped schema with `source` and `external_id`; Vrbo/Stayz iCal-only, so partial reservations exist, a manager can create or complete a booking, and statements refuse to close on incomplete ones. (3) Invite-only email six-digit OTP via Resend SMTP, one auth user per person, `memberships` with roles in the JWT via the Custom Access Token hook, RLS on every table and `storage.objects`, role from session never URL, co-owners and multi-property owners from day one, a founder may be manager and owner, guests have no account. (4) Tenant and long-term letting out of v1; enum values reserved; no rent, bond or inspection tables. (5) One maintenance entity and machine (reported, quoted, waiting_owner, approved or declined, booked, done, invoiced; under-threshold skips waiting_owner); per-owner threshold with amount and threshold snapshot and timestamp on every approval; quotes and invoices written by the tradie. (6) One threads and messages model with adapters (Hospitable, Twilio SMS and voice, Resend inbound, in-app); an outbox driven by an event, role, channel, fallback, quiet-hours matrix; `first_human_reply_at`; the Claude assistant (claude-sonnet-5-5) only drafts; the published number is a Twilio AU mobile. (7) Per-stay door codes in Supabase Vault, revealed server-side only inside the window (default from the property's code release time, 2pm, on arrival day to checkout plus grace), audit row per reveal, per-job crew codes; lock hardware later; v1 manager enters codes. (8) Supabase ap-southeast-2; Netlify with Functions region `syd` on Pro; photos resized in the browser (1600 px JPEG), private bucket, signed URLs, 24-month retention; PDFs by `@react-pdf/renderer` in a route handler. (9) Every history view reads the append-only `events` table. (10) Next.js 16 conventions from `node_modules/next/dist/docs`; no `proxy.ts`; session refresh in a route handler; `@supabase/ssr` with publishable and secret keys.

What genuinely needs the founders (default in brackets): notice period after the first Easter (30 days both ways); whether an Easter a few weeks after go-live counts (yes); repairs billing, tradie invoices the owner or Tidewell pays and passes through (direct; Tidewell-paid items are disbursements at cost; ask the accountant); cleaning economics (Tidewell pays cleaners and passes each clean through at cost; the guest cleaning fee stays with the owner); the definition of accommodation revenue (gross accommodation charged to the guest, before the platform host fee, excluding cleaning fees and taxes, recognised in the check-in month); collector (Pinch over Stripe BECS); cleaner model (ABN contractors with super and TPAR; Fair Work position reviewed); the land agent licence (onboarding gated by `org_settings.onboarding_open` until confirmed); co-owner signatures (any one); signature method (in-app click-to-sign); lock policy and hardware cost (igloohome, owner pays from the setup fee); night-time ring for red guest issues (yes, both founders after 10 minutes); owner access to wifi and lockbox codes (none unless granted); photos in the handover pack (yes); the enquiry form on the Sydney route handler with Netlify Forms as a disclosed fallback (yes); the email sending domain and who reads replies (no default; email stays off until set).

## 3. Data model

### 3.1 Conventions for every migration

- Schemas: `public` (exposed; tables, views, RPCs) and `private` (not exposed; RLS helpers, secret functions; the door-code spec's `secure`). `config.toml` keeps `schemas = ["public", "graphql_public"]`.
- Keys: browser and `@supabase/ssr` cookie clients use the publishable key plus the session, so RLS applies; the secret key is imported from `lib/supabase/secret.ts` only (ESLint `no-restricted-imports`) and used by the routes in section 6; Edge Functions hold it in their own secrets. Never the legacy anon or service_role JWTs.
- RLS on every table, `to authenticated`, `(select auth.uid())` in predicates; helpers in `private` are `security definer stable` with `search_path` pinned and read `memberships` live so revocation is immediate. The only JWT claim RLS trusts is `is_manager`.
- Write discipline (ship-fast graft): tables carrying snapshots, timestamps of record or state (`approvals, quotes, supplier_invoices, photos, messages, jobs, reservations, maintenance_items, statements, invoices, access_codes, events, outbox, code_reveals`) grant `select` only; every change goes through a `security definer` RPC that checks `auth.uid()` and role, sets the server-side fields and calls `private.emit_event()`. A `before update` guard on each state table raises unless `current_setting('app.rpc', true)` names the calling RPC.
- Immutability triggers (`before update or delete`, raise for every role including the secret key) on `events, approvals, code_reveals, disclosure_acknowledgements` and issued `statements, invoices, documents`.
- Owner and crew subsets of sensitive tables are `security_invoker` views; the base table denies that role. Every policy-filtered column is indexed; "one active X per Y" rules are partial unique indexes.
- Defaults in the column lists below: `id uuid pk default gen_random_uuid()` unless stated; `*_at` is `timestamptz`; `*_cents` is `int` (bigint on statement and invoice totals); `x→t` means `references t(id)`; `bool` defaults false; every table has `created_at` and mutable tables `updated_at` (shared `set_updated_at` trigger).
- Realtime: `postgres_changes` is never enabled; a trigger on `events` calls `realtime.broadcast_changes()` to private topics `property:{id}`, `user:{id}`, `managers`; `realtime.messages` policies mirror table policies.

### 3.3 Tables

## 4. State machines

Every transition is one RPC or trigger in one transaction, writes one `events` row through `private.emit_event()`, and leaves fan-out to `route_event()`. Guard triggers raise on anything not in the matrix or not from the named RPC.

**Reservation.** (new) → confirmed by the Hospitable adapter (`upsert_reservation()`) after the supersede rule (a confirmed iCal row on the same property with overlapping dates becomes superseded and its jobs move to the new id in the same transaction); (new) → confirmed incomplete by the iCal poller (dates only, managers told, turnover derived at once); (new) → confirmed by a manager; (new) → tentative by an owner's `request_owner_block()`, confirmed by `confirm_owner_block()`. incomplete → complete by `complete_reservation()` (guest, money, platform, real times; "no contact" must be recorded if no handle). Dates change in place (exclusion constraint; the job moves; a pushed non-editable code is superseded); grants only through `grant_late_checkout()` and `grant_early_checkin()`, refused with `TURNOVER_TOO_TIGHT` unless a manager forces. → cancelled by the adapter (Hospitable cancel, or a UID absent from two consecutive successful polls) or a manager: job cancelled or orphaned, code and stay link revoked.

**Clean job.** `derive_turnovers(property)` is idempotent and runs after every reservation change, grant, reclassification or default-time change and nightly at 02:00 local over today minus 1 to plus 365 days. For each occupancy D (confirmed, guest or owner): `window_start_at` = effective checkout; A = the occupancy with the earliest effective check-in strictly after D's; `ready_by_at = window_end_at = least(A effective check-in, A's arrival date at code_release_time)` if A exists, else D's checkout date at `default_ready_by_time` (or start plus `turnover_minutes` if later); `is_same_day`; `window_flag` overlap or tight; `scheduled_date` the checkout date. Reconcile on `departing_reservation_id`: missing → insert scheduled with the `job_rooms` snapshot and `bill_to`, auto-offer the default cleaner; unstarted with a different window → move, `job.rescheduled`, crew told on a date change or a 60-minute move (pinned jobs only get `job.window_conflict`); started → untouched, `job.window_conflict`; D gone → cancel unstarted jobs (`reservation_cancelled`) or orphan started ones with pay standing; cancelled jobs are never revived (`supersedes_job_id`). Manager: `offer_assignment` (null default pay forces an amount), `cancel_job(reason)` (owner_declined_clean and manager_cancelled set `clean_suppressed`), `reschedule_job`, `pin_job`, `create_manual_job` (manual, refresh, qa_reclean, deep, inspection; never derived). Crew: accept or decline (`job.late_decline` within 24 h of start), `start_job` or first photo → in_progress, `block_job('guest_present')` (urgent to the manager, status unchanged), `submit_job_completion(job, manifest, note, declared_at)` → `ROOMS_MISSING` or `ROOMS_NOT_CONFIGURED`, else done when every manifest photo is current, else done_pending_upload; each `upsert_room_photo()` re-evaluates and the first satisfying upload sets `done_at`. Entering done, and only then: `job.done`, photos and note visible to the owner, one pay line per offered-or-accepted assignment, `locked_at = least(arriving check-in, done_at + 48 h)`, `qa_status = review` on time flags, reopens, a cleaner's first three jobs at the property or `qa_every_clean`. Cron: stale at 2 h, blocked at 24 h (`mark_done_with_exception` or reopen). `reject_photo` and `reopen_job` only before `locked_at` (`JOB_LOCKED` after; a qa_reclean job instead); pay lines are only ever withheld with a reason. Trade jobs run scheduled, assigned, accepted, in_progress (before photo), done (after photo via `complete_maintenance()`).

**Maintenance.** reported by `report_issue()` (manager, owner on their property, crew with a job there, or from a thread). reported or declined → quoted by `submit_quote()` (assigned tradie or manager): quote row, previous superseded, `threshold_cents_at_quote` from the account; in the same transaction, under threshold → `approvals(kind under_threshold)` and approved (`maintenance.auto_approved`), else waiting_owner (`maintenance.waiting_owner`: in-app, push or SMS now, always an email with the quote; reminders 24 h and 72 h; stalled to the manager at 72 h). waiting_owner → approved or declined by an owner member via `decide_approval()` writing `approvals(kind owner)` with snapshots (`unique (quote_id)` makes repeat taps idempotent; co-owners' reminders cancelled; tradie paged) or by `manager_override_approval()` (phone approval; owner told). approved → booked by `book_maintenance()` (trade job; owner, tradie and a resident guest told). booked → done by `complete_maintenance(after_photo)`. done → invoiced by `submit_supplier_invoice()` (tradie with ABN or supplier statement) or `attach_supplier_invoice()`: addressee from `org_settings`, `due_at` = after photo + 7 days when addressed to Tidewell, variance versus the snapshot, `needs_review` above the larger of 10 per cent or A$50; disbursements wait for the next statement; owner-addressed invoices become informational lines. Any pre-done state → cancelled by `cancel_maintenance(reason)`.

**Thread.** open on the first inbound (adapter, `ask_question()` or the enquiry handler; unknown senders get a contact); drafted by `assistant-draft` on guest threads (nothing sent); any inbound → needs_person (always in v1) with `sla_due_at = roll_into_hours(sent_at) + sla_minutes` (paused 22:00-08:00), red pages managers at priority 0, one acknowledgement per party per 12 hours; → replied by `send_message()` from a person (outbound row, `kind = 'message'` outbox row on the thread's channel, `first_human_reply_at` if null, `sla_met`, acknowledgements; owner threads also `thread.human_reply`); cron every 5 minutes emits `sla_at_risk` at 45 minutes (SMS all managers, once per cycle) and sets `sla_breached_at` past due; `take_over_thread()` pauses the assistant; `resolve_thread()` ends it; a later inbound reopens with a fresh cycle.

**Statement and invoice.** draft by cron on the 1st at 02:00 Adelaide or `build_statement()`: reservations assigned by `fee_schedules.revenue_recognition` (check-in month) with check-in on or after `billing_start_date`; lines for accommodation, cleaning fee, platform fee, taxes, paid_to_owner from matched payouts (or expected payout labelled as such), `management_fee = round(accommodation × bps / 10000)`, gst, disbursement_repair, disbursement_clean (done jobs billed guest_fee or owner_statement), owner_direct, credit, adjustment. draft → blocked with `blocked_reasons`: an incomplete reservation; a job in the period in_progress, reopened or pending over 24 h; an unmatched or variance payout; an unconfirmed disbursement; null legal name or ABN; no primary contact. ready → issued by `issue_statement()`: totals frozen, invoice row (sequence number, snapshots, `fee_schedule_id`, lines, due in 7 days), `pg_net` POST for both PDFs, `statement.issued` (email, in-app, routine push; never SMS), `schedule_debit()` if a mandate is active else `invoice.manual_collection`. issued → superseded by `supersede_statement()` (new version; old invoice voided with a credit line). Invoice: draft → issued → debit_scheduled (`invoice.scheduled` 3 days ahead) → paid or failed (`invoice.dishonoured`; three re-schedules then a call task); issued or failed → paid by `record_manual_payment()`; void only through supersede or `void_invoice()`.

**Property.** (lead won) → onboarding by `convert_lead()` (blocked while `onboarding_open` is false). `refresh_steps()` recomputes each step from its evidence row: agreement_signed (both signatures, stored PDF, every disclosure key), owner_access (an owner's `last_sign_in_at`; waivable), direct_debit_active (collector-written active mandate), channel_connected (adapter-written `connected_at` plus `hospitable_property_id`; waivable to ical_only), vrbo_ical (import ok and export fetched; auto-waived without a vrbo listing), fact_sheet_complete (no missing key, a room, a wifi secret when the SSID is set), access_ready (`property_access` plus an active standing or crew code; waivable to key_only), baseline_inspection_done (a done inspection job with a current photo per required room); setup-only homes use agreement_signed, fact_sheet_complete, access_ready, listing_created, baseline_inspection_done, handover_pack_delivered and end at handed_over. onboarding → live by `go_live(property, confirm)` only when `can_go_live()` is empty: `live_at`, `billing_start_date`, `agreements.earliest_exit_date` = the day after Easter Monday of the first Easter Sunday strictly after `live_at`; derivation starts for check-ins on or after `live_at`. live → offboarding by `start_offboarding()` (owner member or manager; `effective_date >= greatest(today + notice_days, earliest_exit_date)` unless a manager waives with a reason) with steps O1 future stays acknowledged, O2 jobs wound down, O3 codes and later stay links revoked at 23:59 on the date, O4 channels disconnected by the adapter (imports stopped, vrbo exports revoked, scheduled Hospitable messages removed first), O5 final statement (`is_final`; same incomplete guard; done when paid, written off or dispute-resolved), O6 mandate cancelled only after O5 and only with no other live or offboarding property, O7 handover pack (done on download or 30 days plus a resend). offboarding → closed by `close_property()` when `can_close()` is empty: calendar token revoked, crew memberships ended, owner memberships read_only for 90 days when no property remains, retention clocks start (secrets at +30 days, guest data at +12 months, financial documents 5 years). `cancel_offboarding()` before the date returns to live. Health flags while live (connection error after 24 h of silence plus a failed backfill; `billing_blocked` on a failed mandate) never change status.

**Access code and stay link.** Guest window: arrival date at `code_release_time` (14:00, decision 7; the door-code spec's "check-in time" is this setting) to `check_out_at + checkout_grace_minutes` (120); crew window: job start minus 60 to end plus 120 minutes. A code is active (manual entry or standing copy; later adapter-pushed no earlier than 48 h before `valid_from`), superseded (date change or re-entry; non-revocable hardware codes get `still_live_until`), revoked, expired (derived) or destroyed (+30 days; metadata and reveals kept 24 months). Reveal is not a state change: `reveal_for_stay()` (service role), `reveal_access_code()` and `reveal_property_secret()` check role, link or assignment, window and the 30-per-hour limit, write the `code_reveals` row first, then return the value. `stay.code_ready` at `valid_from` carries the link only; `stay.code_missing` at T-24 h; `stay.code_missing_live` breaks quiet hours. Stay link: pending → delivered (at check-in minus `stay_link_days_before` via Hospitable, else SMS or email) → active → expired; any → revoked.

**Lead:** new → contacted (first human reply or logged call) → walkthrough_booked → walkthrough_done → proposal_sent (blocked while `onboarding_open` is false) → won; lost and parked need reasons and are reachable from any stage except won; duplicates within 90 days append a note. **Outbox row:** queued → sending → sent → delivered or bounced; cancelled with a reason (superseded, digested, acknowledged, no_address, stale); retries at 1, 5, 15, 60 minutes then failed with a fallback rung; unacknowledged rows get the next rung once after `ack_wait_minutes`. **Pay run:** open → approved (refused while a crew member lacks a verified ABN or supplier statement) → exported (post-launch) → paid.

## 7. Adapters

Every external system sits behind an adapter writing Tidewell-shaped rows keyed by `source` and `external_id`, logging inbound calls in `webhook_receipts` and outbound references in `integration_sync`; swapping a provider is a new Edge Function writing the same tables. Hospitable: `reservation.*` to `guests` and `reservations` (platform, dates, guest count, accommodation, cleaning fee, platform fee, taxes, expected payout; payouts to `platform_payouts` where exposed), `message.created` to `threads` and `messages`, `property.*` to `channel_connections` and `channel_listings`; outbound replies and block confirmations; receipts make retries idempotent, a mapping error is a failed receipt with no partial writes, 24 hours of silence plus a failed backfill marks the connection error, the backfill heals the 12-hour message limit, sends are spaced to 2 per minute per reservation, and masked guest contacts make the platform message the default stay-link channel. iCal: VEVENT UID to incomplete guest reservations updated in place and cancelled only after two consecutive successful polls without the UID; the outbound feed serves Vrbo and the owner; vrbo stays on Vrbo Payments so the owner is merchant of record; the double-booking window is disclosed; tokens rotate on offboarding. Twilio: inbound SMS and MMS matched by phone to a profile, guest or contact, voice legs as messages of channel voice, status receipts; `published_phone_e164` is the only published number; failed sends fall back to email; STOP sets `sms_opt_out_at`; a null number disables the channel. Resend: Supabase Auth SMTP for codes (custom SMTP before the first owner), outbound with React Email templates and signed links, inbound matched by sender or the `[TW-xxxx]` subject token; bounces fall back to SMS; an SMTP outage alerts a manager. Payment collector (`lib/server/collector.ts`: `createCustomer`, `createMandateLink`, `scheduleDebit`, `cancelDebit`, `parseWebhook`; Pinch default, Stripe BECS alternative): no mandate means the invoice issues as manual collection; dishonours allow three re-schedules then a call task; a switch is an `org_settings` value plus a second implementation; money only ever flows owner to Tidewell for Tidewell's own invoice. Xero (post-launch): sales invoices with PDFs, bills for Tidewell-addressed supplier invoices and approved pay lines, payments, contacts; idempotent on `integration_sync`; an outage never blocks issuing. Lock hardware (post-launch; igloohome direct, Seam for other brands): codes issued from `private.issue_lock_code()` and written to Vault like manual codes; device webhooks into `events` and `locks`; creation failure alerts 24 hours before arrival; non-revocable codes raise `still_live`. Claude Property Assistant: drafts only from guest-audience facts with a cached prompt and structured output; errors leave `needs_person`; an unchanged draft a person sends is signed "Property Assistant (automated)". Web push (VAPID): 404 or 410 sets `failed_at` and the next rung fires. Netlify Forms: fallback copy only, deduplicated by `netlify_submission_id`.

## 11. Risks

1. Land agent licensing: if CBS confirms holiday letting is land agent work even under Model A, onboarding is gated; `onboarding_open` is the pause switch.
2. Guest contact masking on Airbnb and Vrbo makes the stay link and code-ready message depend on Hospitable messaging and a tapped link; iCal-only guests have no channel until a manager records one; the promise to owners must be worded for it.
3. iCal-only Stayz makes partial reservations normal; uncompleted ones stall statements and show "details to come"; the daily nag is a mitigation, not a fix.
4. Hospitable dependency for the dashboard and the Airbnb inbox; the adapter design keeps a swap possible, but an outage degrades the inbox to SMS and email.
5. Netlify issue #3575 is open; this design avoids `proxy.ts`, but a later developer following Supabase's guide could add it and break deploys; the plugin is pinned and declared.
6. The secret key bypasses RLS; the eight-file import boundary, pgTAP and a review rule on those files are the controls.
7. Vault rows do not survive a plain dump restore (root key in the runbook); dashboard access bypasses the reveal audit (MFA, two named accounts).
8. App-scheduled, checklist-driven cleaners may be employees under the Fair Work test; super and TPAR are real costs inside the 18% that the schema records but does not pay.
9. Direct-debit dishonours from owners two hours away will happen; a persistently failing owner has no remedy beyond a call and offboarding.
10. Repairs billing is undecided; until settled the crew copy "paid within seven days" is only true for invoices addressed to Tidewell.
11. Moonta Bay reception: the queue makes the photo promise survivable, but a cleaner leaving before uploads finish delays the owner's notification, and library uploads can game evidence (time flags and QA are the controls).
12. Privacy: interior photos, guest names and owner income are personal information; the small-business exemption is likely to lapse and the privacy tort already applies; retention jobs, the audience column, the breach plan and the privacy page are compliance features.
13. Scope: 58 tables and a dozen Edge Functions for one developer and two founders; the phase plan and the post-launch cut list are the control, and vendor account delays are the likeliest slip.
14. Most vendors bill in USD and several prices came from secondary sources; confirm in the consoles before quoting owners.
15. The SA short-stay register and council approvals may add fees, numbers or rate changes within 12 to 24 months; the compliance panel and the `levy` line kind exist so neither needs a rebuild.
