# Tidewell backend design

> Version 1.1 records the changes from the three review passes. Unchanged table columns, enum values, event types, state-machine steps, RPC lists, screen wiring and tests are in `backend-design-v1.0-base.md`; where the two disagree, this file wins.

Version 1.1, 7 October 2026. Starts from the operations proposal (highest judged score), grafts the ideas the judges flagged from the ship-fast and owner-trust proposals, fixes the fatal flaws they named, folds in the four specs, and applies the three review passes of 7 October 2026 (attacker; accountant and compliance; developer feasibility). Section 3 is written so migrations can start tomorrow. A longer working copy with every spec rule in full sits at `(working notes, not in the repo)`.

Conventions: money is integer cents (AUD); timestamps are `timestamptz`; day boundaries are evaluated in the property's timezone (`Australia/Adelaide`, DST-aware). Nothing here invents a business fact: phone, ABN, legal name, email domain, licence number and the Twilio number are `null` in `org_settings` until the founders set them, as `lib/site.ts` does today.

## 1. Summary for Shannon and Court

One database in Sydney that every screen reads and writes, replacing the demo data and browser-only state: your owners and homes, every booking from every channel, every clean and its photos, every repair with its quote and the owner's decision, every message on every channel, every statement and invoice, every door code (encrypted). The screens stay; each is rewired to real rows, and the role a person sees comes from their login, never the web address.

| Promise on the website | What makes it true |
|---|---|
| Published prices | The 18% + GST (19.8% all in), the setup band and the pass-through rules are a `fee_schedules` row the owner's agreement points at; every invoice follows the agreement's row, never the latest one. |
| A live dashboard | A Hospitable webhook is re-fetched and becomes a `reservations` row within seconds; the owner's phone refreshes live; Stayz (iCal) bookings land within 30 minutes as "details to come"; payout figures are labelled expected until a real payout is matched. |
| A photo of every room after every clean | A clean snapshots its rooms; the database refuses to mark it done until a photo exists for each; a recorded photo can never be overwritten; photos queue on the phone without reception. |
| Replies inside the hour, 8am to 10pm | Every inbound message opens an SLA cycle; the first reply by a person closes it; you are paged at 45 minutes; breaches are counted per inbound message, not per conversation. |
| No lock-in after the first Easter | The agreement carries `earliest_exit_date`; the owner can give notice from their phone once it passes; leaving hands back statements, invoices and photos. |
| We never hold your money | Platforms pay owners and the schema refuses any other payee; one monthly tax invoice per owner collected by direct debit; the statement reconciles payouts against it; a claim a platform pays is the owner's receipt; no netted payout exists anywhere. |

Build order: 0 Foundations (one login with a second factor for managers, role from session, Sydney, audit log, privacy page and collection notices); 1 Owners and homes; 2 Bookings in; 3 Turnovers and photos; 4 Repairs and notifications; 5 Stay page and door codes; 6 Inbox and the one-hour reply; 7 Money; 8 Offboarding. About 70 developer-days for one senior developer (95 with the post-launch phases); the honest shortcut is to onboard the first owners after phase 5, with the privacy page and manager MFA in place from phase 0, and run the inbox and the first statement by hand while 6 to 8 finish.

Monthly running cost is as in version 1.0 (figures seen 7 October 2026; roughly US$200-250 for five homes plus debit fees and Xero: Supabase Pro US$25, Netlify Pro US$20, Hospitable US$59 plus US$15 per extra home, Twilio US$8.25 plus usage, Resend US$0-20, Claude drafts US$5-20, Pinch about A$3 per invoice and now one debit per owner however many homes, Xero A$78); Turnstile is free.

## 2. Decisions assumed, and the short list that needs you

The ten decisions taken are as given, with these refinements from the reviews: (1) one tax invoice per owner account per month, each home a section; a "disbursement" is only a supplier invoice addressed to the owner that Tidewell paid on their behalf, and anything Tidewell is itself invoiced for (every clean, any repair addressed to Tidewell) is re-charged as Tidewell's own taxable supply with GST on top; a claim a platform pays is the owner's receipt. (2) Hospitable webhooks are hints: every entity is re-fetched by id before a write. (3) JWT claims route the UI only; RLS reads memberships live; managers carry a TOTP second factor from phase 0; codes expire in ten minutes behind Turnstile and password sign-in is refused by hook. (5) a quote at or under the threshold skips waiting_owner once per item; an invoice above what was approved needs a variance approval; inbound email never advances a repair. (6) the one-hour reply is measured per inbound cycle; the assistant only drafts and only suggests a level. (7) a code is revealed only while its reservation is confirmed; crew can reveal only their own per-job code. (8) Edge Functions are pinned to Sydney; a photo is immutable once recorded; PDFs come from a durable outbox job. (9) non-internal event payloads are whitelisted per type. (10) Server Components verify the access token locally and never refresh it; `@supabase/ssr` cookies keep their default, browser-readable options.

What genuinely needs the founders (default in brackets): notice period after the first Easter (30 days both ways, published on the site FAQ from the `fee_schedules` row the agreement reads); whether an Easter a few weeks after go-live counts (yes); repairs billing (the tradie invoices the owner and Tidewell pays on their behalf as an agency disbursement with no GST uplift; a tradie who invoices Tidewell is re-charged with GST on top; the accountant confirms the default, disclosed as `repairs_billing`); cleaning economics (each clean re-charged as Tidewell's own taxable supply, cleaner pay plus super where it applies plus GST, published as `clean_charge_basis`; the alternative is absorbing cleaning in the 18%; "at cost with no GST" is unavailable because Tidewell contracts the cleaner, not the owner); whether the published setup band is GST-inclusive (yes); accommodation revenue (gross accommodation before the platform host fee, excluding cleaning fees and taxes, in the check-in month); fee on a cancellation payout the host keeps (no); an overpayment on a corrected invoice (carried forward as a credit; refund on request); Tidewell lodging a platform claim in its own name (no); passing on the dishonour fee (no); collector (Pinch over Stripe BECS); cleaner model (ABN contractors with super paid per pay run and a TPAR; Fair Work position reviewed); the land agent licence (gated by `onboarding_open`; number and licensee null until confirmed); co-owner powers (any member signs; a threshold increase or notice by a non-primary member waits 24 hours while every member is told and may be undone); in-app click-to-sign; igloohome, owner pays from the setup fee; night-time ring for red guest issues (yes, both founders after 10 minutes, capped per thread and per unknown caller; red is set by a person, never the assistant); owner access to wifi and lockbox codes (none unless granted, with the disclosed exception that a stay link sent as an Airbnb message is readable in the host's Airbnb inbox); photos in the handover pack (yes); Netlify Forms as a signature-verified enquiry fallback (yes); the email sending domain and who reads replies (no default); the emergency repair cap a manager may approve alone (none until set).

## 3. Data model

### 3.1 Conventions for every migration

- Schemas: `public` (exposed; tables, views, RPCs) and `private` (not exposed; RLS helpers, secret functions; the door-code spec's `secure`). `usage` on `private` is revoked from `anon`, `authenticated` and `service_role` and granted only to `postgres` and, for the token hook, `supabase_auth_admin`. Extensions live in `extensions`, so every pinned `search_path` is `pg_catalog, public, extensions`.
- Keys: browser and `@supabase/ssr` cookie clients use the publishable key plus the session, so RLS applies; the secret key is imported from `lib/supabase/secret.ts` only (ESLint `no-restricted-imports` in `eslint.config.mjs`, since `next lint` is gone in 16) and used by the routes in section 6; Edge Functions read it from `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEYS` and, because it is not a JWT, run with `verify_jwt = false` and authorise in code. Never the legacy anon or service_role JWTs.
- RLS on every table, `to authenticated`, `(select auth.uid())` in predicates; helpers in `private` are `security definer stable` with `search_path` pinned and read `memberships` live, so revocation is immediate and no JWT claim is trusted for authorisation. `private.is_manager()` requires an active manager membership and `auth.jwt()->>'aal' = 'aal2'`; `private.is_owner_of(p)` (reads) accepts `active` and `read_only` owner memberships on the account that owns `p`, `private.is_active_owner_of(p)` (every write RPC) accepts `active` only, and `private.my_owner_account_ids()` follows the same split; `private.crew_at(p)` is an active crew membership plus an offered or accepted assignment on a non-cancelled job at `p` whose window lies within now minus 14 days to now plus 30 days; `private.can_see_job(j)` is an active crew membership plus an offered or accepted assignment on `j`. Every policy carries its scoping predicate; a bare role check is a review failure, and pgTAP seeds two owner accounts and asserts a cross-account negative select per table.
- Write discipline: state and snapshot tables (`approvals, quotes, supplier_invoices, photos, messages, jobs, reservations, maintenance_items, statements, invoices, access_codes, events, outbox, code_reveals, thread_sla_cycles`) grant `select` only; every change goes through a `security definer` RPC that checks `auth.uid()` and role, sets the server-side fields and calls `private.emit_event()`; a `before update` guard raises unless `current_setting('app.rpc', true)` names the calling RPC. Free text that reaches a guest or crew member (facts, `notes_for_crew`, `entry_notes`, outbound bodies, rendered notifications) passes `private.lint_secret_text()`, which refuses a run of 4 to 8 digits and the words code, pin and password unless the RPC is given an `allow_digits_reason`.
- Immutability triggers (`before update or delete`, raise for every role including the secret key) on `events, approvals, code_reveals, disclosure_acknowledgements, thread_sla_cycles` and issued `statements` (except `reconciled_at`), `invoices` and `documents`. Row triggers do not fire on `TRUNCATE`, so `truncate` is revoked on every public table from `anon`, `authenticated` and `service_role` (with `alter default privileges`); `usage` and `select` on `invoice_seq` are revoked from `anon` and `authenticated`; pgTAP asserts each grant.
- Column hiding uses one model: owner, crew, tradie and public subsets are plain views (not `security_invoker`) owned by `postgres`, `security_barrier`, with the role predicate in the view body; the owner bypasses the base table's RLS, the base table grants nothing to the role the view serves, and `select` on the view goes to `authenticated` (and `anon` for the two public views). Supabase lint 0010 is suppressed for exactly the views in 3.6. Fields a customer or crew member must never read live in manager-only side tables (`owner_account_notes`, `thread_internal`). pgTAP per view: the role it serves gets zero rows or an error from the base table. Policy-filtered columns are indexed; "one active X per Y" rules are partial unique indexes.
- Column defaults are as in version 1.0 (`id uuid pk default gen_random_uuid()`, `*_at timestamptz`, `*_cents int`, `x→t` for `references t(id)`, `created_at` and `updated_at`).
- Realtime: `postgres_changes` is never enabled. `events_route` calls `realtime.send(jsonb_build_object('entity_type', .., 'entity_id', .., 'type', ..), 'refresh', topic, true)` with no payload (clients only `router.refresh()`) to audience-split private topics `property:{id}:owner`, `property:{id}:crew`, `managers`, `user:{id}`. `realtime.messages` select policies match `realtime.topic()`: `:owner` through `is_owner_of`, `:crew` through `crew_at`, `managers` through `is_manager()`, `user:{id}` through `auth.uid()`; no `insert` policy, so no browser can broadcast. Realtime re-authorises only at join and token refresh, so `end_membership()` deletes the person's `auth.sessions` rows. pgTAP: an owner's select on `realtime.messages` for `property:{id}:crew` returns nothing.

### 3.2 Enums

The version 1.0 enums stand except: `bill_to: owner_invoice, owner_stay, none`; `approval_kind: owner, under_threshold, variance, emergency_override`; `payout_source: reservation_financials, manual, csv`; `statement_line_kind: accommodation, cleaning_fee, platform_fee, taxes, paid_to_owner, expected_payout, management_fee, clean_charge, disbursement_repair, recharge_repair, owner_direct, credit, adjustment, levy, dishonour_fee`; `invoice_line_kind: management_fee, clean_charge, disbursement_repair, recharge_repair, setup_fee, credit, adjustment, levy, dishonour_fee` (no `gst` line kind anywhere; GST is a per-line amount and an invoice total); `invoice_status` adds `credited, refunded`; `document_kind` adds `adjustment_note`; `outbox_kind` adds `document`; `deny_reason` adds `pending_cancel, superseded`; `cleaning_model: recharge, absorbed`. New: `claimant: owner, tidewell`; `payout_kind: booking, cancellation, claim, adjustment`; `invoice_kind: monthly, setup`; `webhook_verification: signature, refetch, none`; `clean_charge_basis: pay, pay_plus_super, fixed`. Notification priority stays `smallint check (priority in (0,1,2))`: 0 urgent, 1 important, 2 routine.

### 3.3 Tables

"Manager" means `private.is_manager()`; "owner of P" means `private.is_owner_of(P)` for reads and `private.is_active_owner_of(P)` for writes; "members of A" means `private.my_owner_account_ids() @> array[A]`; "crew at P" and "crew on J" are `private.crew_at(P)` and `private.can_see_job(J)`. RLS shorthand: **P** = select manager or owner of `property_id`, writes manager; **A** = select manager or members of `owner_account_id`, writes manager; **M** = manager only; **child(t)** = as the parent row `t` through `exists`. A `*_owner`, `*_crew`, `*_tradie` or `*_public` view is a definer view under 3.1 and the base table has no policy for the role it serves.

#### Organisation and identity

**org_settings** (single row; nulls are unconfirmed and hidden). Columns as in version 1.0 minus `default_notice_days`, plus `land_agent_licence_number text, licensee_user_id→profiles` (null until CBS confirms), `privacy_policy_version, collection_notice_version text`, `emergency_override_cap_cents int default 0`, `tidewell_may_claim bool default false`, `pass_on_dishonour_fee bool default false`; `gst_registered` must be true before any `issue_*` RPC runs; `default_supplier_invoice_addressee` is disclosed as `repairs_billing`. RLS: M; every other role, including `anon`, reads `org_settings_public` (published fields, hours, timezone, licence number).

**fee_schedules** (published prices as rows; a change is a new row only new agreements point at). `effective_from date unique`, `management_fee_bps int (1800)`, `gst_bps int (1000)`, `total_fee_inc_gst_bps int generated (1980)` shown beside "18% + GST" wherever the fee appears, `setup_fee_min_inc_gst_cents (300000)`, `setup_fee_max_inc_gst_cents (500000)`, `fee_base text default 'accommodation_cents'`, `revenue_recognition default 'check_in'`, `cleaning_model default 'recharge'`, `clean_charge_basis default 'pay_plus_super'`, `owner_stay_clean_at_cost bool default true`, `cancellation_payout_fee_bps int default 0`, `default_notice_days int default 30`, `fee_definition text not null` (direct bookings are paid to the owner; Tidewell runs no checkout and takes no co-host payout), `passthrough_definition text not null` (cleaning basis, repairs billing, dishonour fee, cancellation payouts, each with its GST treatment), `published_url, published_at, created_by`. RLS: M; `fee_schedules_public` (all but `created_by`) to `anon` and `authenticated`; update and delete none.

**profiles** (one per auth user; never the source of role). `id→auth.users on delete cascade`, `full_name, first_name text not null` (from `user_metadata`; `/api/members` must pass it or the trigger aborts user creation), `initials generated`, `email citext unique` (mirrored by the `auth.users` trigger), `phone_e164, phone_verified_at, sms_opt_out_at, email_bounced_at, timezone, is_active bool default true, mfa_enrolled_at`. RLS: select own row or manager only; other roles reach names only through views (`jobs_owner`, `reservations_crew`, and `profiles_directory`: first names of managers and the caller's co-assignees on open jobs; no email or phone); update own name, phone, timezone or manager; insert by trigger only. `sms_opt_out_at` is set by STOP only from a `phone_verified_at` number, confirmed by push or email.

**crew_profiles**. `user_id pk→profiles`, `crew_kind`, `business_name, abn text check 11 digits, abn_verified_at, entity_type (sole_trader|company|partnership|trust), gst_registered bool, super_applies bool default true, address jsonb` (TPAR), `super_fund jsonb` (member number as a Vault secret id), `supplier_statement_document_id→documents, rcti_agreement_signed_at, bank_details_secret_id uuid (Vault), xero_contact_id`. An `rcti` is produced only when `gst_registered` and `rcti_agreement_signed_at` are both set; otherwise a `remittance` advice. RLS: select self or manager; insert manager; update manager (self may set business name, address and ABN before verification).

**owner_accounts** (the billing party; co-owners share one; may own several homes; one invoice per account per month). `display_name text not null, legal_name, abn, gst_registered bool, billing_email citext not null, billing_address jsonb`, `approval_threshold_cents int default 15000` (inc GST; a quote at or under it auto-approves), `threshold_pending_cents, threshold_pending_until` (an increase by a non-primary member waits 24 hours while every member is told), `engagement_type default 'managed'`, `pm_user_id→profiles`, `contact_method default 'phone'`, `contact_cadence default 'monthly'`, `prefs text[]`, `credit_balance_cents bigint default 0` (carry-forward from corrected invoices), `xero_contact_id`, `created_from_lead_id→leads`, `started_at date, ended_at date`. RLS: A, except `set_approval_threshold()` and `set_contact_preferences()` by an active owner member; insert `convert_lead()` or manager; delete none.

**owner_account_notes** (manager-only notes about the customer). `owner_account_id pk→owner_accounts cascade, notes text, relationship_level level default 'green', billing_blocked bool, billing_blocked_reason, last_human_contact_at` (maintained by a trigger on `interactions`), `updated_by→profiles`. RLS: M; delete none.

**memberships** (who may act as what; `property_id` is reserved for tenants). `user_id→profiles cascade`, `role app_role`, `owner_account_id→owner_accounts cascade`, `property_id→properties cascade`, `crew_kind`, `status membership_status default 'invited'`, `secrets_access default 'none'`, `is_primary_contact bool`, `invited_by→profiles, invited_at, accepted_at, ended_at, confirmed_by→profiles, confirmed_at`. Checks: owner needs `owner_account_id`; crew needs `crew_kind`; manager has neither. `unique (user_id, role, owner_account_id, property_id) nulls not distinct`; partial unique one primary contact per account among invited or active rows. RLS: select own rows, manager, or rows on an account the caller belongs to; a `to supabase_auth_admin` select policy for the token hook; writes manager only through `add_member()` / `end_membership()`. A manager row becomes `active` only when a different manager calls `confirm_member()` (two-person rule; the founders are created in the dashboard); a trigger refuses self-granted manager rows. `end_membership()` sets `ended_at` and deletes the person's `auth.sessions` rows (so the next refresh and Realtime renewal fail); `memberships_after_end` removes their open assignments, cancels queued offers and deletes push subscriptions.

## 4. State machines

Every transition is one RPC or trigger in one transaction, writes one `events` row through `private.emit_event()`, and leaves fan-out to `route_event()`. Guard triggers raise on anything not in the matrix or not from the named RPC.

**Reservation.** (new) → confirmed by the Hospitable adapter (`upsert_reservation()`, always from the entity re-fetched by id) after the supersede rule (a confirmed iCal row with overlapping dates becomes superseded; its jobs, stay link and guest codes move to the new id in the same transaction, codes reissued when dates changed); (new) → confirmed incomplete by the iCal poller; (new) → confirmed by a manager; (new) → tentative by `request_owner_block()`, confirmed by `confirm_owner_block()`. incomplete → complete by `complete_reservation()`. Dates change in place; grants only through `grant_late_checkout()` and `grant_early_checkin()`. Cancellation: a re-fetched Hospitable cancel cancels at once; an iCal UID missing from one poll sets `pending_cancel_at` (reveals denied, managers told) and a second miss cancels, but only while `check_in_at - 48 h` is ahead; after that a vanished UID is never auto-cancelled (feeds drop past events; a stayed, paid booking must not leave the fee base): `ical.vanished` pages managers, the row stays confirmed and incomplete, and only a manager cancels it with a reason, which blocks the statement until `confirm_cancellation()`. Any cancellation cancels or orphans the job and revokes code and stay link.

**Clean job.** Derivation, reconciliation, assignment, completion, QA and lock are as in version 1.0 (`derive_turnovers` idempotent after every reservation change and nightly; `submit_job_completion` → `ROOMS_MISSING` or done or done_pending_upload; `locked_at = least(arriving check-in, done_at + 48 h)`), with these changes: `bill_to` is `owner_invoice` after a guest stay, `owner_stay` after an owner stay, `none` when the owner declined the clean; a photo attested by a row is never overwritten, so a retake is a new `client_photo_id`; entering done writes one pay line per assignment (`amount_cents`, `gst_cents` for a registered cleaner, `super_cents` where super applies) and the `clean_charge` the owner's next invoice carries under `clean_charge_basis` with GST on top; the 2-hour stale nudge goes to the cleaner by push then SMS; `notes_for_crew` passes the secret lint; a cleaner whose membership ends drops off every job.

**Maintenance.** reported by `report_issue()` (manager, active owner, crew at the property, or from a thread). reported or declined → quoted by `submit_quote()`: quote row, previous superseded, `threshold_cents_at_quote` from the account; the quote auto-approves in the same transaction (`approvals(kind under_threshold)`) only when `total_inc_gst_cents <= threshold`, no earlier auto-approval or over-threshold quote exists on the item, and the quote is not within 10 per cent under the threshold unless a manager has called `confirm_quote()`; otherwise waiting_owner (in-app, push or SMS now, always an email; reminders 24 h and 72 h; stalled to the manager at 72 h). waiting_owner → approved or declined by an active owner member via `decide_approval()` (`approvals(kind owner)` with snapshots; `unique (quote_id)` makes repeat taps idempotent; co-owners' reminders cancelled; tradie paged), by a manager logging the owner's telephone decision (`via phone_logged`; every member told and able to dispute), or, for `urgent` items under the emergency cap, by `emergency_override_approval(reason)`. approved → booked by `book_maintenance()`. booked → done by `complete_maintenance(after_photo)`. done → invoiced only through `confirm_supplier_invoice()`: `submit_supplier_invoice()` or `attach_supplier_invoice()` creates the row (inbound email creates a `needs_review` row only, and only with SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment or a `[TW-xxxx]` token matching the assigned tradie's address); the manager picks owner-addressed disbursement (no GST) or Tidewell-addressed recharge (GST on top); `due_at` = after photo + 7 days when addressed to Tidewell; when `total_inc_gst_cents` exceeds the greater of the approved amount and the threshold, the item goes done → waiting_owner with `pending_invoice_id` and a `variance` approval for the excess, returning to done → invoiced on approval or leaving the invoice `disputed` on decline. Disbursements and recharges wait for the next statement; owner-addressed invoices Tidewell did not pay become `owner_direct` lines. Any pre-done state → cancelled by `cancel_maintenance(reason)`.

**Thread.** open on the first inbound (unknown or unverified senders get a contact); drafted by `assistant-draft` on guest threads (nothing sent; its level is a suggestion in `thread_internal`); any inbound → needs_person and opens a `thread_sla_cycles` row with `due_at = roll_into_hours(sent_at) + sla_minutes` (paused 22:00-08:00) unless one is open; red, set by a manager or a reviewed keyword rule, pages managers at priority 0 with voice at most once per thread per hour and once per unknown contact per night; → replied by `send_message()` from a person (outbound row signed with the person's first name, the open cycle closed with `met`, `first_human_reply_at` if null, `threads.sla_met` mirroring the cycle); cron emits `sla_at_risk` at 45 minutes of the open cycle and sets `breached_at` past due; `take_over_thread()` pauses the assistant; `resolve_thread()` ends it; a later inbound opens a fresh cycle, so a long conversation can meet the hour ten times and miss it once, and the miss is counted.

**Statement and invoice.** draft by cron on the 1st at 02:00 Adelaide or `build_statement()`: reservations by `revenue_recognition` (check-in month) from `billing_start_date`, the fee schedule from the property's agreement; lines for accommodation, cleaning fee, platform fee, taxes, `paid_to_owner` from matched payouts and `expected_payout` for the rest (`has_expected_lines`), `management_fee = round(accommodation × bps / 10000)`, `clean_charge`, `recharge_repair` (GST on top), `disbursement_repair` (no GST), `owner_direct`, `credit` (Tidewell's own goodwill or a supersede reversal only), `adjustment`, and claim and cancellation payouts among the payout lines. draft → blocked with `blocked_reasons`: an incomplete reservation; a job in the period in_progress, reopened or pending over 24 h; an unconfirmed supplier invoice; an unconfirmed post-check-in cancellation; null legal name or ABN, or `gst_registered` not true; no primary contact. An unmatched or variance payout is a `warning`, never a block (payouts are entered by hand for most sources and the fee does not depend on them). ready → issued per owner account by `issue_statements(account, period)` once every live property of the account has a ready statement: totals frozen, one invoice row for the account (sequence number, snapshots, the agreement's `fee_schedule_id`, lines carrying `statement_id` and `property_id`, `gst_cents` as the sum of line GST, due in 7 days), `statement.issue_requested` plus an outbox `document` job per PDF; the owner is told (`statement.issued`: email, in-app, routine push; never SMS) only when `record_document()` has stored both PDFs; `schedule_debit()` if a mandate is active, else `invoice.manual_collection`. issued → superseded by `supersede_statement()` (new version; old invoice voided; if `paid` or `debit_scheduled`, an `adjustment_note` PDF plus a carry-forward credit or `collector.refund()`). `reconciled_at` is set when every guest reservation in the period is matched, including by a payout dated the following month. Invoice: draft → issued → debit_scheduled (`invoice.scheduled` 3 days ahead) → paid or failed (`invoice.dishonoured`; three re-schedules then a call task); issued or failed → paid by `record_manual_payment()`; paid → credited or refunded only through a supersede; `void_invoice()` refuses on a paid invoice without an adjustment note.

**Property.** Steps, `can_go_live()`, `go_live()` (with `earliest_exit_date` = the day after Easter Monday of the first Easter Sunday strictly after `live_at`), the offboarding steps O1 to O7 and `close_property()` are as in version 1.0, with these changes: `start_offboarding()` by an active owner member who is not the primary contact carries a 24-hour `undo_until` during which every member is told on every channel and any member may cancel; O3 also rotates wifi and lockbox codes; `close_property()` leaves owner memberships read_only for 90 days, during which reads continue through `is_owner_of` and writes stop because every write RPC uses `is_active_owner_of`; guest data retention is held while a claim or open item references it; `billing_blocked` lives in `owner_account_notes`.

**Access code and stay link.** Windows and lifecycle are as in version 1.0 (guest: arrival date at `code_release_time` to `check_out_at + checkout_grace_minutes`; crew: job start minus 60 to end plus 120 minutes; destroyed at +30 days, metadata and reveals kept 24 months). Reveal is not a state change: `reveal_for_stay()` (secret key), `reveal_access_code()` and `reveal_property_secret()` write the `code_reveals` row first, then return the value, and otherwise deny with a reason: the parent reservation must be `confirmed` (not `superseded`, `cancelled` or carrying `pending_cancel_at`); a Hospitable row whose `last_webhook_at` and `last_backfill_at` are both older than two hours is re-checked through the API first; a guest reveals only through a live stay link inside the window, at most 30 times per link per hour; crew reveal only `kind = 'crew'` rows whose `job_id` is one of their accepted assignments, inside the crew window, and `guest` and `standing` kinds always answer `role`; a manager reveals with a reason; an owner never reveals a code. `showWifi` is gated to the door-code window, a crew wifi reveal to the crew window. Stay link: pending → delivered (at check-in minus `stay_link_days_before` by SMS or email when a verified guest handle exists, else as a Hospitable message, shown to the owner as "sent via Airbnb message") → active → expired; any → revoked; on a supersede the link follows the new row.

**Lead:** new → contacted → walkthrough_booked → walkthrough_done → proposal_sent (blocked while `onboarding_open` is false; the proposal cites the published `fee_schedule_id`) → won; lost and parked need reasons; duplicates within 90 days append a note and send no second acknowledgement. **Outbox row:** queued → sending → sent → delivered or bounced; cancelled with a reason; retries at 1, 5, 15, 60 minutes then failed with a fallback rung; unacknowledged rows get the next rung once after `ack_wait_minutes`; `document` rows complete when their `documents` row exists. **Pay run:** open → approved (refused while a crew member lacks a verified ABN or supplier statement) → exported (post-launch) → paid, with `record_super_payment()` before the next run opens.

## 7. Adapters

Every external system sits behind an adapter writing Tidewell-shaped rows keyed by `source` and `external_id`, logging inbound calls in `webhook_receipts` and outbound references in `integration_sync`; swapping a provider is a new Edge Function writing the same tables. Hospitable: the help article documents the events, five retries backing off 1 s, 5 s, 10 s, 1 h and 6 h, and the 12-hour message limit, but no payload signing; signing is confirmed with Hospitable support before phase 2 and, absent one, each connection carries a random path token in its webhook URL and every payload is only a hint re-fetched by id with the PAT before writing, deduped on `webhook_receipts`, with the backfill sized for the 6-hour final retry; expected figures go to `platform_payouts` as `reservation_financials`, never as actual payouts; a listing whose payee is not the owner is refused; a mapping error is a failed receipt with no partial writes; sends are spaced to 2 per minute per reservation; masked guest contacts make the platform message the fallback stay-link channel, which the owner panel labels. iCal: UIDs to incomplete guest reservations with the pending-cancel and vanished rules of section 4; vrbo stays on Vrbo Payments so the owner is merchant of record. Twilio: inbound matched by phone to a `phone_verified_at` profile, a guest or a contact (an unverified number lands as a contact); STOP honoured only for a verified number and confirmed by push or email; no SMS text ever approves, accepts or cancels anything. Resend: Auth SMTP for codes, React Email templates, inbound trusted only with SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment and producing `needs_review` rows only; bounces fall back to SMS. Payment collector (`lib/server/collector.ts`: `createCustomer`, `createMandateLink`, `scheduleDebit`, `cancelDebit`, `refund`, `getPayment`, `getMandate`, `parseWebhook`; Pinch default, Stripe BECS alternative; every webhook event re-fetched): no mandate means manual collection; dishonours allow three re-schedules then a call task; money only ever flows owner to Tidewell for Tidewell's own invoice, and back only as a refund of it. Xero (post-launch): sales invoices, bills for Tidewell-addressed supplier invoices and approved pay lines, payments, contacts; idempotent on `integration_sync`. Lock hardware (post-launch; igloohome direct, Seam for other brands): codes issued from `private.issue_lock_code()` into Vault; non-revocable codes raise `still_live`. Claude Property Assistant: drafts only; its level is a suggestion; a message a person sends is signed with that person's name. Web push (VAPID): 404 or 410 sets `failed_at` and the next rung fires. Netlify Forms: fallback copy only, accepted through the signed outgoing webhook and deduplicated by `netlify_submission_id`.

## 8. Next.js integration

Read against Next 16.3.6: middleware is `proxy.ts` and this design has none; `next.config.mjs` carries only `headers()` for `/stay/:token*` and `serverExternalPackages: ['@react-pdf/renderer']` (fontkit and yoga are Node-only and must stay external under Turbopack), so Cache Components are off and `force-dynamic` still applies; a Content-Security-Policy limits scripts to the app's own origin, Supabase and, on the enquiry form only, Turnstile; Server Functions are reachable by direct POST, so every one calls an RPC that authorises itself; the repo gains `eslint.config.mjs` with `eslint-config-next`.

Auth flow: `/app` (server) renders the sign-in form; the demo picker and `lib/demo.ts` imports go. Email posts to `send-code` (`signInWithOtp`, `shouldCreateUser: false`, Turnstile verified by Supabase's captcha setting; template `{{ .Token }}`; `otp_expiry = 600`; one request per 60 seconds, "wait a minute" on 429; the same response for known and unknown addresses). `/app/verify` posts the six digits to `verify` (`verifyOtp`, `type: 'email'`); the SSR client writes cookies with the `@supabase/ssr` defaults, `httpOnly: false`, `secure`, `sameSite = 'lax'`, because the browser client reads the session from `document.cookie` and the private Realtime subscriber, the cleaner's direct Storage upload and the offline queue's `upsert_room_photo()` all need it (with `httpOnly` cookies those three run as `anon` and fail); XSS is answered by the no-third-party-script rule and the CSP. A Password Verification hook returns `decision: reject` for every password sign-in, so `updateUser({ password })` cannot open a second door; manager sessions are timeboxed. Redirect to `/app`, which reads the verified claims and `memberships`: a manager membership routes first to `/app/mfa` (`mfa.challengeAndVerify` with the TOTP factor enrolled at first sign-in; nothing manager-only renders until the session is `aal2`), one role lands on `/app/{role}`, several on `/app/choose` (the choice is a cookie, not a URL privilege), none shows "your login is not set up yet, ring us". The layouts, `SessionProvider`, manifest, sign-out and the removal of `roleFromPath()`, `useAppState` and `app/app/tenant/**` are as in version 1.0.

Session refresh without `proxy.ts` (opennextjs-netlify issue #3575, open): a Server Component never triggers a refresh, because `getClaims()` without a token calls `getSession()`, which refreshes within 90 seconds of expiry, and a Server Component cannot write the rotated cookies (`cookies().set` throws, the pattern swallows it, the browser keeps the old refresh token and the next call fails with `refresh_token_already_used`, signing the person out). Instead `lib/auth.ts` reads the `sb-*-auth-token` cookie, verifies the access token locally with `jose` against the project's JWKS (asymmetric signing keys enabled; key set cached), and treats a missing or expired token as "needs refresh": `redirect('/api/session?next=<path>')`. `/api/session` (route handler, cookie-writing client) refreshes, sets cookies and redirects back, or returns 204 when called by the client `SessionKeeper` on mount, on `visibilitychange` and every 20 minutes. `getClaims()` and `getUser()` are used only in Server Actions and route handlers. Route protection is layered as the bundled docs say: the layout check guards the shell only, because layouts do not re-render on client-side navigation, so `requireRole()` (cached per request with React `cache()`) is also the first line of every page's data loader in `lib/data/*` and of every Server Action; a revoked membership loses data on the next request and the stale shell on the next navigation. `@netlify/plugin-nextjs` stays declared and pinned. Playwright: expire the access token, hit a Server Component route, confirm no sign-out; end a membership and soft-navigate, confirm the data is gone.

Server versus client: reads are Server Components calling one read RPC per page or typed queries through the cookie client, every loader starting with `requireRole()`; live lists mount a small client subscriber on the audience-split private topic that only calls `router.refresh()`, so no client queries sensitive tables and no payload crosses the socket; writes are Server Actions calling RPCs then `refresh()`; the cleaner's photo flow is the one client-heavy path (IndexedDB queue, direct Storage upload under `storage.objects` RLS with the browser session, then `upsert_room_photo`; Netlify never sees a photo); signed URLs come from the two sign routes by row id; `supabase gen types typescript` replaces the hand-written types.

Screen rewiring is as in version 1.0 with these changes: `/app/mfa` enrols and challenges the manager's TOTP factor; `/app/more` shows the collection notice, "takes effect in 24 hours; the other owners have been told" on a non-primary threshold increase, and the undo window on "Leaving Tidewell"; `/app/owner/bookings` reads `ical_exports_owner` and `code_reveals_owner` (the "who had access" panel, including "sent via Airbnb message"); `/app/owner/repairs` reads `maintenance_items_owner`, `photos_owner` and variance requests; `/app/owner/money` shows "expected" badges until `reconciled_at`, one invoice per account with a section per home, "Your net after Tidewell's invoice" with "Repairs billed to you directly" beneath it, and credits carried forward; `/app/crew/job/[id]` reads `access_codes_crew` and `maintenance_items_tradie`; `/app/crew/pay` reads `pay_runs_crew`; `/inbox` reads `thread_internal` and the assistant's suggested level; `/money` shows `warnings`, invoices per account, adjustment notes and super payments; `/owners` reads `owner_account_notes` and writes `confirm_member`; `/human-touch` reads `thread_sla_cycles`; the site reads `fee_schedules_public` (18% + GST, the 19.8% total, the setup band, the notice period, the pass-through definition) and links the privacy page.

## 11. Risks

1. Land agent licensing: if CBS confirms holiday letting is land agent work even under Model A, onboarding is gated; `onboarding_open` is the pause switch.
2. Guest contact masking makes the stay link depend on Hospitable messaging and a tapped link; iCal-only guests have no channel until a manager records one.
3. iCal-only Stayz makes partial reservations normal; uncompleted ones stall statements; the daily nag is a mitigation, not a fix.
4. Hospitable dependency for the dashboard and the Airbnb inbox; an outage degrades the inbox to SMS and email.
5. Netlify issue #3575 is open; this design avoids `proxy.ts`, but a later developer following Supabase's guide could add it and break deploys; the plugin is pinned and declared.
6. The secret key bypasses RLS; the eight-file import boundary, sign routes that accept only row ids, pgTAP and a review rule on those files are the controls.
7. Vault rows do not survive a plain dump restore (root key in the runbook); dashboard access bypasses the reveal audit (MFA, two named accounts).
8. App-scheduled, checklist-driven cleaners may be employees under the Fair Work test; super and TPAR are real costs the schema records per pay run; the accountant confirms the Payday Super timing and the `pay_plus_super` basis of the clean charge.
9. Direct-debit dishonours will happen; a persistently failing owner has no remedy beyond a call and offboarding.
10. Repairs billing has two explicit paths (owner-addressed disbursement without GST, Tidewell-addressed recharge with GST) and the manager picks one per invoice; until the accountant confirms the default, the crew copy "paid within seven days" is only true for invoices addressed to Tidewell.
11. Moonta Bay reception: the queue makes the photo promise survivable, but a cleaner leaving before uploads finish delays the owner's notification (time flags and QA control library uploads).
12. Privacy: interior photos, guest names, reveal logs and owner income are personal information; the privacy page and collection notices ship in phase 0 and name every overseas processor (Anthropic, Twilio, Resend, Hospitable, Pinch or Stripe, Apple and Google push) and the two automated decisions (under-threshold auto-approval and the assistant's suggested level, whose transparency grace period ends 10 December 2026).
13. Scope: 62 tables and a dozen Edge Functions for one developer and two founders; the phase plan and the post-launch cut list are the control, and vendor account delays are the likeliest slip.
14. Most vendors bill in USD and several prices are from secondary sources; confirm in the consoles before quoting owners.
15. The SA short-stay register and council approvals may add fees, numbers or rate changes within 12 to 24 months; the compliance panel and the `levy` line kind exist so neither needs a rebuild.
16. Hospitable's webhook signing is unconfirmed; the path token and the re-fetch rule make a spoofed webhook harmless, but the 6-hour final retry means a missed event can be up to a day late before the backfill heals it.
17. A stay link delivered as an Airbnb message is readable in the host's own Airbnb inbox, so "who had access" cannot attribute a guest reveal with certainty for those bookings; the panel discloses it and SMS or email delivery is preferred whenever a verified handle exists.
18. Manager MFA from phase 0 adds friction for two founders on the road; a lost authenticator is recovered by the other founder through the dashboard, and recovery codes are kept offline.
19. The GST-inclusive setup band and the 19.8% total are published to meet component-pricing rules; if the founders decide the band is ex GST, the columns are renamed and the site copy changes before the first proposal, never after.
